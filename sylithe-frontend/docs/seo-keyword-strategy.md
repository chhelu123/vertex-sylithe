# Sylithe SEO & Keyword Strategy (India-first, dMRV/MRV)

Source: Google Trends (India + Worldwide, Jul 2025–Jul 2026) + intent analysis.

## The core insight
Search demand is dominated by **buyer / price / "what is" / India** intent.
**"dMRV" and "MRV" have almost no search volume** — do NOT target them as primary
keywords. Win the high-volume carbon-credit terms with education content, then funnel
readers to `/platform` (dMRV) and `/for-buyers`. That is how Sylithe becomes *known*
for dMRV in India — by owning the carbon-credit conversation, not the acronym.

## On-page rules (apply to every page/post)
- Lead the `<title>` and H1 with the primary keyword (not "dMRV").
- One primary keyword per page; don't cannibalize (each term owned by one URL).
- Unique meta description (~150 chars) with the primary keyword + India.
- Internal-link from high-traffic blogs → `/platform` and `/for-buyers` (money pages).
- FAQ schema on guides (targets "People Also Ask").
- Keep every new post registered in `blogData.js` (auto-added to sitemap → prerendered → indexed).
